I am an incoming Ph.D. student at NLP, MBZUAI, working with Asst. Prof. Xiuying Chen. My research is dedicated to building trustworthy machine learning systems and exploring their applications in various sub-tasks.

I obtained my Master degree at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Ran He. I was a research intern at the TMLR Group, Hong Kong Baptist University, working with A.P. Bo Han. I am also closely collaborating with Dr. Junchi Yu and Dr. Qizhou Wang.
